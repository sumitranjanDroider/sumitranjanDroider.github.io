1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines the practices and policies of Sumit Ranjan ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, or use our mobile applications distributed via the Google Play Store and other authorized platforms.

This policy applies to all independent mobile applications published under our developer account, including but not limited to AI Habit Tracker, Study Routine / Habit Tracker, AI Notes, and other mobile utility software. By downloading or using our applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

We are committed to minimizing data collection. The types of data processed depend on how you interact with individual applications:

  • User-Provided Content: Notes, habit definitions, routine schedules, checklists, and preferences you input into our applications are primarily stored locally on your device unless explicit cloud synchronization features are selected.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: When you use our applications, basic technical data such as device manufacturer, model, operating system version, crash diagnostics, and performance metrics may be collected automatically by third-party SDKs to diagnose bugs and maintain application stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Non-sensitive identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (AAID) may be accessed by advertising partners to deliver relevant advertisements in supported ad-funded applications.

Key Principle: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information to any third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

Some of our free mobile applications may be supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process information about your device, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Google AAID)
  • IP address and approximate coarse location
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, and impressions)

These third-party ad networks use this information to serve relevant, personalized or non-personalized advertisements, monitor ad delivery, and prevent fraudulent ad traffic. We implement authorized digital seller standards, including AdMob app-ads.txt verification, to protect the integrity of the advertising supply chain.

You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking at any time through your Android device settings by navigating to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

5. Device Permissions

Our applications may request certain system permissions to enable specific features. Permissions are only requested when needed:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to load advertisements, verify app licensing, and sync cloud data if enabled.
  • Notification Access (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to deliver user-scheduled routine reminders, habit alerts, and study timers.
  • Camera Access (CAMERA): Utilized exclusively by Document Scanner and AI Expense Manager to capture document scans and receipts locally on your device. Camera streams are processed in memory and never transmitted without explicit user consent.
  • Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to save scanned PDF documents and export habit/financial reports directly to your local file system.

You have full control over these permissions and can grant or revoke them at any time in your device's application settings.

6. App-Specific Disclosures

Each mobile application is designed with user privacy at its core:

AI Habit Tracker (Package: com.desidroider.studyplanner)

View Dedicated App Policy →

All daily habit records, streak counts, timetable entries, and custom study routines are stored locally in your private on-device sandbox. For detailed information regarding Google Sign-in authentication, Google Drive cloud backups, and data deletion requests, please refer to the dedicated AI Habit Tracker Privacy Policy.

AI Expense Manager

Personal financial data, monthly budget thresholds, transaction histories, and scanned receipts remain stored privately on your device. No bank account login credentials or sensitive payment details are ever requested or stored.

Document Scanner

Document images, camera snapshots, and generated PDF files are processed and stored locally on your device. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) operates on-device, ensuring confidential documents never leave your phone.

AI Notes

Your notes, text entries, and personal markdown documents are maintained in local storage. When AI summarization is invoked, text is processed strictly for the requested summary without training public models on your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the event that we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers and records. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
